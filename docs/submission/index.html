The problem

Farmers decide with last year's weather

Same country, different reality

In one week Azua received +103 % of its normal rainfall while Constanza received +3 %. A national average would hide both farms.

Data does not reach the field

Satellites produce global layers; the farmer needs an answer for his plot: what to plant, when to irrigate, what risk he faces.

Rotation choices involve water, soil health, pests, markets and risk — a crop that helps one farm can hurt another.
Source: Open-Meteo/ERA5 (non-NASA), own 1991–2020 normal2 / 7

What we built

One screen that opens on the farm

Real satellite map

Three demo plots over real imagery, with sensors, roof and camera labelled as simulated.

NASA observations

NASA POWER climate and risks (heat, heavy rain, dry days, frost, root-zone moisture, evapotranspiration), SRTM terrain, MODIS NDVI, GPM IMERG.

Local soil + crop traits

ISRIC SoilGrids texture, carbon and pH (non-NASA, labelled); crop water demand and critical months.

Farmer priorities

Sliders for water saving and diversification, plus a rainfall scenario to see which strategy holds up.

Farmer-first design

Plain language, a phone, and a real bot

No jargon

“127 L/m²” instead of “126.8 mm”; “green 65 out of 100” instead of NDVI 0.65; a field glossary inside the app.

Real Telegram bot

@Eliofarmbot answers /estado, /clima, /techo on and free questions using only the farm's data.

Mobile and automatic

Pinch-zoom map, automatic irrigation below 25 % soil moisture and roof closing with real ERA5 rain.

Honest labels

Sensors, roof and camera are simulated and said so; sources marked NASA or non-NASA.

Works without internet: self-contained demo5 / 7

Validity and limits

What we know, and what we do not

  • Uncertainty shown: FDR correction over 2,592 grid cells, bootstrap confidence intervals, adjusted p-values.
  • Satellite vs ground: NASA POWER is reanalysis; MODIS pixel ~289 m and IMERG ~2.3 km, larger than a 1 ha plot — explained on screen.
  • Declared gaps: bare-field calendar, pest cycles, local market and agronomic validation are not covered yet.
  • Roadmap: SMAP, ECOSTRESS, GEDI, GRACE and Landsat are next; they need Earthdata access and pixel harmonisation.
Support tool: it does not replace farmer judgment or local agronomic expertise.
